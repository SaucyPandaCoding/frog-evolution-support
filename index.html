Frog Evolution: Pond Panic

Privacy policy and player support

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS app Frog Evolution: Pond Panic, provided by Stefan Grigoras.

Data collection

The app does not collect or transmit personal information, gameplay data, or device identifiers to the developer. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics services. No account is required.

Progress stored on your device

High scores, coins, upgrades, and outfit selections are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Deleting the app may erase this progress.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

Support and privacy questions

You can contact the developer through the support issue tracker . Issues are public. Do not post personal information, passwords, or other sensitive details. GitHub handles information you provide there under its own privacy policy .

Changes

This page will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change. The effective date will identify the latest policy.

Player Support

Report a problem or ask a question . A GitHub account is required to post.

For bugs, include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. Avoid screenshots containing personal information.

Progress is saved locally. Before troubleshooting, try closing and reopening the app. Avoid deleting it if you want to retain your progress.
