Support

For a bug report, purchase issue, or privacy question, open a support issue. Include your app version, iPhone model, and a description of what happened.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post passwords, payment details, receipts, transaction identifiers, private contact details, or other sensitive information. A GitHub account is required to create an issue.

For Apple billing or refund requests, visit Apple’s Report a Problem service.

Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026. Developer: Stefan Grigoras, publishing as SaucyPandaCoding.

Scope

This policy describes the iOS app’s local progress storage, optional rewarded advertising, and Apple in-app purchases. TestFlight builds may show test ads and sandbox purchases; available features can vary by build. This policy does not cover other apps or third-party websites you choose to visit.

Game progress

The app stores game progress, high scores, coins, unlocks, settings, and purchase-delivery records locally on your device. These records are used to operate the game and prevent duplicate delivery of purchased coins. The app does not upload your game save to a developer-operated account or cloud-save service. Progress is not guaranteed to survive uninstalling the app or moving to another device.

Rewarded advertising and Google

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform for optional rewarded ads and privacy choices. The non-personalized-ad configuration requests contextual rather than interest-based ads, disables the publisher first-party identifier, and does not request permission to access the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking.

Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is collected. Google’s SDK may receive:

  • Your IP address, which can indicate approximate location.
  • Device or app-related identifiers, where available under the platform and privacy settings.
  • Advertising activity, such as ads shown, ad interactions, and video views.
  • Product interactions, crash logs, and performance information such as launch time or energy usage.

Google may process this information to deliver and measure ads, provide analytics, prevent fraud, and maintain or improve SDK reliability. The developer does not operate a separate behavioural analytics service in the app.

Read Google’s SDK data disclosure, Google’s Privacy Policy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for details of Google’s processing, retention, and available controls.

Choices

Rewarded ads are optional. You can decline an ad and continue using the game without that ad’s reward. The in-app Privacy control opens Google’s privacy choices where they are required and available. Some regions require consent even for non-personalized ads; an ad may be unavailable when applicable consent requirements are not met.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. The app receives verified transaction information, including the product and transaction identifier, to deliver coins and avoid duplicate delivery. It does not receive your payment-card number or bank details. Apple’s handling of billing and account information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Support information

If you contact us through GitHub, we use the information you voluntarily provide to respond and investigate your request. GitHub hosts those communications under its own privacy policy. Please disclose only what is needed to explain the issue.

Retention and deletion

Local app records remain on your device until removed through app or device controls. Removing the app generally removes its local data, but device backups may retain copies. Deleting local records does not delete Apple’s transaction records or information already processed by Google. Those providers apply their own retention and deletion policies. Contact us through the support link for questions about information you supplied directly to us; do not post sensitive details publicly.

Children

The app does not ask players to provide their name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. It is not marketed as a service specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, contact us through the support channel without posting the child’s information publicly.

Changes

We will update this page when the app’s privacy practices change and revise the effective date above.
